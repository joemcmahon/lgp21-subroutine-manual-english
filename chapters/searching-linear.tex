\section{Linear Searching}
\subsection*{SEARCH PROGRAM 1 --- K3-10.0}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{SEARCH PROGRAM 1 --- K3-10.0}

\subsubsection*{PURPOSE}
The program K3-10.0 searches a specified memory region for a specific argument, which can be either a decimal address or a hexadecimal constant.

\subsubsection*{STORAGE REQUIREMENTS}
\textbf{Memory:} 1 track, 37 sectors \\
\textbf{Temporary storage:} none

\subsubsection*{OPERATION}
Press \textsf{MAN INPUT} on the Flexowriter tape reader and jump to $L_0$.

\subsubsection*{INPUT}
The program prints the following letters and requests answers to them via the keyboard of the typewriter:

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{cl}
\toprule
\textbf{Letters} & \textbf{Input} \\
\midrule
\texttt{i} & Starting address of the region to be searched. \\
\texttt{f} & Ending address of the region to be searched. \\
\texttt{a} & Argument. If \textsf{PST} is textsf{OFF}, enter a decimal address. \\
           & If \textsf{PST} is \textsf{ON}, enter a hexadecimal constant. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsubsection*{OUTPUT}
\textbf{\textsf{PST OFF}:} \\
If the address part of a cell in the region to be searched is identical to the entered address, a carriage return is executed, and the address of the cell is printed in decimal, followed by a space character and the instruction contained within it. Negative instructions are preceded by a minus sign.

\noindent \textbf{\textsf{PST ON}:} \\
A carriage return is executed and the address of the cell is printed.

\subsubsection*{OPERATION}
When the search is completed, the computer stops at $L_0 + 27$. Pressing \textsf{Start} restarts the program.
